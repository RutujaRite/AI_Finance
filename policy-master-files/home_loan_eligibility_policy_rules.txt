2. JANA SMALL FINANCE BANK – SELF-EMPLOYED LAP / OD
------------------------------------------------
Product:
- Self-employed LAP and OD / MSE funding.

Loan Amount:
- ₹50 lakh to ₹25 crore.

Interest / LTV:
- ROI shown starting from 10.75% fixed.
- LTV up to 85%.

Eligible Profiles / Programs:
- Self-employed / MSE profiles.
- Banking Surrogate Program.
- Professional Program.
- RTR Program.

Banking Surrogate Program:
- Eligibility up to ₹5 crore.
- Multiple bank accounts considered.
- CA and SA accounts accepted.
- Up to ₹3 crore: 1.25x ABB to EMI.
- Up to ₹5 crore: 1.5x ABB to EMI.
- ABB means Average of 365 days / latest 12 months EOD balance of CA/SA accounts.

RTR Program:
- 12-month track: 1.25x of existing EMI.
- 18-month track: 1.5x of existing EMI.
- 24-month track: 1.75x of existing EMI.

Professional Program:
- Eligibility based on cash profit multiplier.
- Doctors (MD/MS/MBBS): 4x.
- Homeopathy/Ayurveda/MDS/Dentist: 2.5x.
- CA and Architect: 3x.
- Cash profit = Net Profit after Tax + Depreciation.

Eligible Property Types:
- Residential.
- Commercial.
- Industrial.
- School/College.
- PG/Multi-unit.
- Warehouse.
- Hospital.
- Cinema/Theatre.
- Other property types as accepted under the program.

Note:
- The supplied image is primarily for self-employed LAP/OD/MSE funding, not a standard salaried home-loan policy.

7. ELIGIBILITY CHECKING FIELDS
------------------------------------------------
For an automated home-loan eligibility check, compare the applicant against the applicable lender policy using:

- Bank / lender.
- Loan product.
- Requested loan amount.
- Applicant profile: salaried / self-employed / professional / business / NRI where supported.
- Monthly income or applicable income measure.
- Existing EMI obligations.
- FOIR, where specified by lender.
- CIBIL / credit profile, where specified.
- Loan tenure.
- LTV, where specified.
- Property type.
- Property purpose: purchase / construction / resale / balance transfer / top-up.
- Income assessment program, such as banking surrogate, RTR, gross turnover, gross receipts, rental income, structured income, or professional multiplier.
- Profession, where a lender program has profession-specific multipliers.
- Required bank-account/ABB criteria, where applicable.

FINAL RULE:
- Do not mark a borrower eligible solely because one condition matches.
- A lender should be treated as potentially eligible only when the applicant satisfies all applicable conditions available in that lender's supplied policy data.
- If a required policy condition is not available in this file, do not invent it. Mark the criterion as "Policy data not provided".
