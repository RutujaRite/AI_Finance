================================================================================
L&T FINANCE PERSONAL LOAN - MASTER POLICY
================================================================================

BANK: L&T Finance
PRODUCT: Personal Loan

================================================================================
1. TARGET SEGMENT
================================================================================

- Salaried individuals only.
- NRI profiles are not allowed.
- Partnership firms are not allowed.
- Proprietorship firms are not allowed.

================================================================================
2. COMPANY CATEGORIES
================================================================================

Company categories:
- Super CAT A
- CAT A
- CAT B
- CAT C
- CAT D
- CAT E / Others

Company category must be resolved from the L&T company master.

================================================================================
3. AGE
================================================================================

- Minimum Age: 21 years
- Maximum Age: 60 years at loan maturity

================================================================================
4. MINIMUM NET MONTHLY SALARY
================================================================================

AGE <= 30 YEARS

Metro:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 35,000

Tier 1:
- Super CAT A / CAT A / CAT B: Rs. 25,000
- CAT C / CAT D / CAT E: Rs. 30,000

Tier 2:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 25,000

Tier 3+:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 20,000

Company Category E:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 40,000


AGE > 30 YEARS

Metro:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 40,000

Tier 1:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 35,000

Tier 2:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 30,000

Tier 3+:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 25,000

Company Category E:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 50,000

================================================================================
5. CIBIL / NTC
================================================================================

Standard CIBIL:
- CIBIL V3 >= 700

NTC / CIBIL -1:
- Allowed under separate NTC policy.

NTC Maximum Loan Amount:
- Super CAT A / CAT A: Up to Rs. 10 Lakhs
- All other categories: Up to Rs. 5 Lakhs

================================================================================
6. LOAN AMOUNT
================================================================================

- Minimum Loan Amount: Rs. 1 Lakh
- Maximum Loan Amount: Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs

NTC:
- Super CAT A / CAT A: Maximum Rs. 10 Lakhs
- Other categories: Maximum Rs. 5 Lakhs

================================================================================
7. TENURE
================================================================================

- Minimum Tenure: 12 months
- Maximum Tenure: 72 months

Income up to Rs. 50,000:
- Maximum Tenure: 48 months

For eligible CAT A / B / C / D customers with salary Rs. 50,000+:
- Tenure may go up to 72 months where applicable.
- Eligibility calculation may be considered on 60-month basis where the
  applicable policy requires it.

================================================================================
8. FOIR
================================================================================

Super CAT A / CAT A / CAT B:
- FOIR: 60% to 80%

CAT C:
- FOIR: 55% to 75%

CAT D / CAT E:
- FOIR: 50% to 70%

High-income policy:
- Salary Rs. 1.5 Lakhs: FOIR up to 70%
- Salary Rs. 2 Lakhs: FOIR up to 80%

ABB:
- Up to 1:1.5

================================================================================
9. ADDITIONAL INCOME
================================================================================

Bonus:
- 50% of credited bonus amount may be considered.

Rental Income:
- Up to 100% may be considered subject to policy and documentation.

Incentive:
- Monthly credited incentive: 100%
- Quarterly incentive: 50%
- Half-yearly incentive: 50%

Rental Income Documents:
- Registered Rent Agreement
- Rent credits in Bank Statement
- Notarized rent agreement is not accepted.

Consultancy Income Documents:
- 26AS or Form 16(A)
- Document should not be more than 15 months old.

================================================================================
11. PROCESSING FEE
================================================================================

Loan Amount Rs. 1L to < Rs. 5L:
- Processing Fee: 2.00%

Loan Amount Rs. 5L to < Rs. 10L:
- Processing Fee: 1.50%

Loan Amount Rs. 10L and above:
- Processing Fee: 1.25%

================================================================================
12. BALANCE TRANSFER
================================================================================

- Only active Personal Loans are eligible for BT.
- Disbursal value must be more than Rs. 1 Lakh.
- Minimum 6 or 12 EMIs must be paid depending on company category.
- Loan must be from an approved financier.

BT Documents:
- Statement of Account
- Repayment Schedule
- Repayment Bank Statement

================================================================================
13. GOVERNMENT EMPLOYEES
================================================================================

Government Teachers:
- Minimum Salary: Rs. 75,000 per month

Grade C & D Employees:
- Not allowed

Grade 4 Employees:
- Not allowed

Government Employees:
- Salary Slip mandatory

================================================================================
14. BANKING / KYC / VKYC REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

Salary Account:
- Must be linked with login mobile number.

Net Banking:
- Must be activated in salary account.

Bank Statement:
- 6 months Salary Account Bank Statement
- Can be provided through:
  * Account Aggregator
  * Net Banking
  * e-PDF upload

eKYC:
- DigiLocker should contain Aadhaar and PAN details.

Mandate Registration:
- Net Banking and/or Debit Card must be active.

VKYC:
- Customer must enable:
  * Microphone
  * Camera
  * Location

Compulsory VKYC Documents:
- PAN
- Aadhaar
- Current Address Proof

Accepted Address Proof:
- Passport
- Driving Licence
- Electricity Bill
- Water Bill
- Mobile Postpaid Bill
- Municipal / Property Tax Receipt
- Rent Agreement with Utility Bill

================================================================================
15. DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

KYC:
- Aadhaar
- PAN

Banking:
- 6 months Salary Account Bank Statement

Address:
- Current Address Proof

Rental Income:
- Registered Rent Agreement
- Rent credits in Bank Statement

Consultancy Income:
- 26AS or Form 16(A), not more than 15 months old

Government Employees:
- Salary Slip mandatory

================================================================================
16. PART PAYMENT / FORECLOSURE
================================================================================

Supplemental policy states:
- Part Payment allowed after 6 EMIs
- Foreclosure allowed after 6 EMIs

Exact charges / limits:
- Not specified in supplied policy material.
