2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
================================================================================

CIBIL Score Requirements:
- Minimum CIBIL: 675
- CIBIL < 700: Loan amount up to ₹5,00,000 only
- CIBIL > 700: Loan amount up to ₹20,00,000
- CIBIL vintage: 6 months mandatory
- CIBIL -1 will be considered for Govt Employees
- New to CIBIL (0/-1): Max loan cap ₹5,00,000
- Max 5 CIBIL enquiries in last 1 month accepted
- No CIBIL enquiries in last 30 days (some versions) [REVIEW]

Age Requirements:
- Minimum Age: 25 years (applicant)
- Co-applicant Minimum Age: 23 years
- Maximum Age: 60 years at time of loan
- Max Age at loan maturity: 70 years (some versions) [REVIEW]

Employment Types:
- Salaried
- Self-Employed

Work Experience / Business Stability:
- Total Experience: Pvt Ltd - 1 year, Govt - 6 months
- Work from Home profile: HR confirmation mandatory irrespective of company categorization
- CAT C/D employer with restricted office entry: HI confirmation mandatory
- CAT A/B employer with restricted office entry: Confirmation email or Chola Staff visit confirming customer employment required
- All non-govt cases: EPFO check and official email ID check mandatory, result to be positive
- All salaried due diligence checks to be ensured
- If norms not met: Empowerment at NCM level
- Office FH can be done by FI agency and Chola employee (COE/BCOM/ACOM/RCOM/ZCOM/RM/SEE/ABM/ASM/RSM/ARM/RRM)

Loan Amount Limits:

- Maximum tenure 48 months for CIBIL score < 750

6. FOIR & MULTIPLIER RULES
================================================================================

FOIR Limits:
NMI Band           | CAT A/Govt/B | CAT C/D
Up to ₹50,000      | 65%          | 55%
Above ₹50,000      | 70%          | 65%

Existing Obligations:
- All loans reflecting in CIBIL, banking and all loans confirmed during PD to be obligated for eligibility calculations
- Any existing loan getting over within 6 months whose loan tenure is >18 months: need not be obligated for arriving total obligations
- Credit Card Obligations: Gross salary * 5 times CC outstanding = Nil obligations
- Nil obligations for Gold loan irrespective of outstanding
- Joint HL not to be obligated if spouse is serving EMI from their account and sufficient to cover all EMIs

7. NTH / SALARY REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

Monthly NTH Requirements:
- General: Above ₹25,000
- Tier 1 Locations: Minimum NMI ₹25,000
- Tier 2 Locations: Minimum NMI ₹20,000
- Salary above ₹40,000: Tenure up to 84 months across all categories

Salary Considerations:
- 3 months Incentive (Average 50%) can be considered for NMI
- Clubbing of income accepted

================================================================================
8. BT / CCBT / APP LOAN RULES
================================================================================

Balance Transfer (BT) Rules:
- Max takeover 6 BT (including CC BT)
- Minimum tenure for BT is 6 EMI
- BT allowed from all banks except co-operative banks/rural banks
- Personal Loan BT FC letter mandatory

Credit Card BT:
- Gross salary * 5 times CC outstanding = Nil obligations

================================================================================
9. DOCUMENT REQUIREMENTS
================================================================================

Mandatory Documents:
- KYC: PAN, Aadhaar (Mask)
- Address proof: AADHAR RECEIPT, Govt ID, Gas Bill and Rental Agreement bond ₹100 with notary with 2 witness signature required
- Ownership proof
- Live Photo (both applicant)
- Banking: 6 months
- Latest 3 months Salary Slip
- Business proof (for Business Loan): GST / Gumasta / Shop & Establishment Act (min 3 years business vintage)
- Professional Degree Certificate / Registration Certificate (SEP)

================================================================================
10. EXCEPTIONS & DEVIATIONS
================================================================================

Bounce & Delinquency Norms:
- No EMI bounce in last 3 months (non-technical)
- 1 bounced allowed in last 6 months

Other Exceptions:
- Govt Grade 4 employees (including Driver, Technician, Sub staff, Attenders & Corporation employees) can be funded up to ₹5,00,000
- Work from Home & Bachelor's: Co-applicant and HR mail confirmation mandatory
- OHP compulsory
- Loan amount between ₹10L to ₹20L: 5 years exp proof mandatory
- No deviation allowed on ROI, PF & Insurance
- Unlisted company not doable
- Manpower & Client location case not doable
