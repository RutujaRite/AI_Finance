BANK: Utkarsh Small Finance Bank

1. COMPANY CATEGORIES
================================================================================

Company Categories:
- CAT A
- CAT B
- CAT C
- CAT D
- CAT E / Unlisted

CAT E:
- Any organization not categorized under CAT A to CAT D
- Prior bank approval required before login where applicable

Company category must be resolved from the Utkarsh Small Finance Bank company list.

================================================================================
2. PERSONAL LOAN - STANDARD PL
================================================================================

Loan Amount:
- Minimum: Rs. 1,50,000
- Maximum: Rs. 10,00,000

CAT E / Unlisted:
- Maximum Loan Amount: Rs. 7,00,000

Tenure:
- Minimum: 12 months
- Maximum: 60 months

Age:
- Minimum: 23 years
- Maximum: 58 years or retirement age, as applicable

CIBIL:
- Minimum CIBIL Score: 650+

Employment:
- Minimum 6 months with current employer
- Total work experience: Minimum 2 years

Banking:
- Salary must be credited directly into applicant's bank account

================================================================================

- Credit Card BT alone is not allowed

Not Taken Over:

BT Documents:
- Any one of:
  - Repayment Schedule
  - Statement of Account
  - Foreclosure Letter

Credit Card BT:
- Latest billing-cycle Credit Card Statement required
- BT amount to be based on outstanding shown in Credit Card Statement

14. EMI / REPAYMENT DATE RULES
================================================================================

Available EMI Dates:
- 1st of the month
- 5th of the month

If loan is disbursed on the 1st:
- First EMI: 1st of succeeding month

If loan is disbursed between 2nd and 5th:
- First EMI: 5th of succeeding month

If loan is disbursed between 6th and month-end:
- First EMI: 1st of the month after the succeeding month
- Pre-EMI interest charged and collected upfront

Insurance:
