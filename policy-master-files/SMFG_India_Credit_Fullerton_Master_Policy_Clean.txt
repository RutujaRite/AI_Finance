================================================================================

- Maximum DBR / FOIR: 60%

- Maximum DBR / FOIR: 65%

NMI Rs. 35,000 and above:
- Maximum DBR / FOIR: 70%

NMI Rs. 35,000 and above:
- Maximum DBR / FOIR: 60%
