6. ABB / EMI / MULTIPLIER RULES
================================================================================

SELF-EMPLOYED ABB-BASED ELIGIBILITY:

Ready Reckoner:
- Serviceable EMI is based on ABB-to-EMI ratio.

ABB Band              | Eligible EMI Ratio
ABB <= 10K            | 45%
ABB >10K to <=30K     | 40%
ABB >30K              | 35%

Scorecard image shows equivalent bands:
- 5K-10K ABB: 45%
- 10K-30K ABB: 40%
- 30K+ ABB: 35%

Exceptions:
- Exceptions to be processed with L4 deviation where explicitly stated.

ABB Calculation:
1. ABB = daily balance average for 6-month statement.
2. Remove non-business credits for eligibility calculation:
   - loan credit
   - self-transfer
   - other non-business credits
3. Multiple bank accounts can be clubbed for ABB calculation.
4. Banking account conduct must be fulfilled by each account individually.
