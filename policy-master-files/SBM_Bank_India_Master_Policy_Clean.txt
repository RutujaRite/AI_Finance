================================================================================
SBM BANK (INDIA) PERSONAL LOAN - MASTER POLICY
================================================================================

BANK: SBM Bank (India)
PRODUCT: Personal Loan

================================================================================
1. ELIGIBILITY
================================================================================

Minimum Salary:
- Listed Company: Rs. 50,000 per month
- Non-Listed Company: Rs. 50,000 per month
- Company approval to be taken prior to login where applicable

Age:
- Minimum: 25 years
- Maximum: 60 years

PF Deduction:
- Mandatory
- Must be verified in Payslip or EPFO Statement

================================================================================
2. CIBIL / NTC
================================================================================

Standard CIBIL:
- Minimum CIBIL Score: 720+

CIBIL 0 / -1:
- Allowed
- Maximum Loan Amount: Rs. 5 Lakhs
- Non-Listed Company: Allowed under the newer policy

================================================================================
3. LOAN AMOUNT
================================================================================

- Minimum Loan Amount: Rs. 5 Lakhs
- Maximum Loan Amount: Rs. 30 Lakhs

Loan Amount Capping:
- Listed Company: Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs
- SBM Group: Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs
- Government: Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs
- Non-Listed Company: Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs
- CIBIL 0 / -1: Up to Rs. 5 Lakhs

================================================================================
4. TENURE
================================================================================

- Minimum Tenure: 12 months
- Maximum Tenure: 60 months

================================================================================
5. FOIR
================================================================================

Salary Rs. 30,000 to Rs. 50,000:
- Maximum FOIR: 50%

Salary above Rs. 50,000 to Rs. 1,00,000:
- Maximum FOIR: 60%

Salary above Rs. 1,00,000:
- Maximum FOIR: 65%

================================================================================
6. CREDIT CARD OBLIGATION
================================================================================

Salary Below Rs. 1 Lakh:
- Credit Card outstanding up to 3 times salary: NIL obligation
- Credit Card outstanding above 3 times salary:
  5% of total outstanding to be considered as obligation

Salary Rs. 1 Lakh and Above:
- Credit Card outstanding up to 5 times salary: NIL obligation
- Credit Card outstanding above 5 times salary:
  5% of total outstanding to be considered as obligation

================================================================================
7. BALANCE TRANSFER
================================================================================

Balance Transfer Allowed For:
- Personal Loan
- Credit Card
- Overdraft
- Jumbo Loan
- App Loan

Tradeline Limit:
- Maximum 5 tradelines including applicable:
  - App Loans
  - Credit Cards
  - Personal Loans
  - Overdrafts

================================================================================
8. PART PAYMENT / FORECLOSURE
================================================================================

Part Payment:
- Allowed after 12 months
- Applicable charge: 4% plus taxes as stated in policy

Foreclosure:
- Allowed after 12 EMIs
- Closure charge: 4% plus taxes

================================================================================
9. PROCESSING FEE / INSURANCE
================================================================================

Processing Fee:
- 2%

Insurance:
- Mandatory

================================================================================
10. CURRENT ROI PRICING GRID
================================================================================

SOURCE: SBM New Pricing Grid - 06-May-2025

Rates are linked to current EBLR and may vary with changes in EBLR.

IMPORTANT:
The source uses the following CIBIL headings exactly:
- CIBIL >750
- CIBIL >720 to <=750
- CIBIL below 730

The last two source labels overlap for CIBIL 721-729.
Do not guess which pricing column applies in that overlap without a verified
clarification.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GRID 1 - NET MONTHLY INCOME > Rs. 1,00,000
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CIBIL >750:
- ROI: 11.25%

CIBIL >720 to <=750:
- ROI: 11.50%

CIBIL below 730:
- ROI: 12.25%

GRID 1 APPLICABILITY:
- Net monthly salary must be above Rs. 1 Lakh
- Loan Amount must be >= Rs. 10 Lakhs
- No App Loan BT
- No Credit Card BT
- Applicant must be employed with a Listed Company
- Private / Partnership employer profiles excluded from Grid 1 as stated
- Employer turnover must be more than Rs. 250 Crores
- Not more than 6 CIBIL enquiries in the last 30 days from login date
- Self-owned house required
- Minimum 5 years stability in the same city

If any Grid 1 condition is not satisfied:
- Do not apply Grid 1 pricing

================================================================================

14. POLICY VERSION NOTES
================================================================================

Older supplied SBM policy versions had different values, including:
- Listed-company salary Rs. 30,000
- Non-listed salary Rs. 50,000
- Age 23 to 58 years
- Minimum loan Rs. 2 Lakhs
- Minimum tenure 6 months
- Non-listed NTC not allowed
- Non-listed maximum funding Rs. 20 Lakhs
- Part payment not allowed
- Older ROI ranges of approximately 13.5% to 16%

The newer SBM PL One Pager dated 12-Mar-2025 and the pricing grid dated
06-May-2025 should be used for the rules above unless the backend has an
explicitly different active policy version.

Do not mix older values with the newer policy.
