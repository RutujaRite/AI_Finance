================================================================================
SBM BANK INDIA PERSONAL LOAN - MASTER POLICY
================================================================================

BANK: SBM Bank India
PRODUCT: Personal Loan
PROFILE: Salaried Individuals
SOURCE: SBM Bank India PL Policy (Digital Co-Lending Program)

================================================================================
1. COMPANY CATEGORIES
================================================================================

Company Categories:
- CAT Super A: Listed Companies, Government entities
- CAT A: Tier 1 / Prime Corporates
- CAT B: Tier 2 / Preferred Corporates
- CAT C: Standard Corporates
- CAT D: Non-Listed / Unlisted Companies

Company category must be resolved from the SBM Bank India company list.

Category-based treatment:
- CAT Super A / Listed Company (Tier 1): Min Salary ₹30,000 | FOIR up to 65% | ROI starts from 11.25%
- CAT A (Tier 1): Min Salary ₹30,000 | FOIR up to 65% | ROI starts from 11.25%
- CAT B (Tier 2): Min Salary ₹30,000 | FOIR up to 60% | ROI starts from 11.50%
- CAT C / CAT D / Non-Listed (Standard): Min Salary ₹50,000 | FOIR up to 50% | ROI starts from 12.25%
- Government: Min Salary ₹30,000 | FOIR up to 65% | ROI starts from 11.25%

================================================================================
2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
================================================================================

Age:
- Minimum: 23 years
- Maximum: 58 years or retirement age, whichever is earlier

CIBIL:
- Minimum CIBIL Score: 720+

Employment Type:
- Salaried individuals only
- Minimum 1 year work experience with current employer

Work Experience:
- Minimum 2 years total work experience
- At least 1 year with current employer

Residence Stability:
- Minimum 6 months at current residence

================================================================================
3. LOAN AMOUNT
================================================================================

Loan Amount:
- Minimum Loan Amount: Rs. 5,00,000
- Maximum Loan Amount: Rs. 30,00,000 (CAT Super A / Listed / Government / Tier 1)
- Maximum Loan Amount: Rs. 25,00,000 (CAT B / Tier 2)
- Maximum Loan Amount: Rs. 20,00,000 (CAT C / CAT D / Non-Listed / Standard)

================================================================================
4. TENURE
================================================================================

Tenure:
- Minimum Tenure: 12 months
- Maximum Tenure: 60 months

================================================================================
5. MINIMUM NET MONTHLY INCOME (NTH)
================================================================================

Listed Company / Tier 1 / Government:
- Minimum NTH: Rs. 30,000

Preferred Company / Tier 2:
- Minimum NTH: Rs. 30,000

Non-Listed Company / Standard Corporate:
- Minimum NTH: Rs. 50,000

Banking:
- Salary must be credited directly into applicant's bank account with regular monthly credits

================================================================================
6. FOIR
================================================================================

Listed Company / Tier 1 / Government:
- Maximum FOIR: 65%

Preferred Company / Tier 2:
- Maximum FOIR: 60%

Non-Listed Company / Standard Corporate:
- Maximum FOIR: 50%

================================================================================
7. ROI GRID - PERSONAL LOAN SALARIED
================================================================================

Applicability:
- ROI depends on Net Monthly Income and Company Category

Net Monthly Income up to Rs. 50,000:
- CAT Super A / Listed Company: 12.50%
- CAT B (Tier 2): 12.75%
- Non-Listed / Standard: 13.50%

Net Monthly Income above Rs. 50,001:
- CAT Super A / Listed Company: 11.25%
- CAT B (Tier 2): 11.50%
- Non-Listed / Standard: 12.25%

Note: SBM Bank operates via digital co-lending program verification.

================================================================================
8. PROCESSING FEE
================================================================================

- Processing Fee: 1.50% of loan amount
- Minimum: Rs. 2,500
- Maximum: Rs. 10,000
- Plus applicable GST and stamp duty

================================================================================
9. DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

Mandatory Documents:
- PAN Card
- Aadhaar Card
- Latest 3 months Salary Slips
- Latest 6 months Bank Statement showing salary credits
- Employee ID Card
- Employment Verification Letter

================================================================================
10. SPECIAL PROFILE RULES
================================================================================

Digital Co-Lending:
- SBM Bank partners with fintech co-lenders for digital underwriting
- Fast-track processing for salaried customers with verified bank statements

Existing Relationships:
- Existing SBM Bank savings/current account holders receive preferential processing

================================================================================
11. EXCEPTIONS & DEVIATIONS
================================================================================

Non-Listed Companies:
- Higher minimum salary requirement (Rs. 50,000+) applies
- Strict FOIR cap of 50% applies
- Review required for approval

Balance Transfer:
- Up to 3 credit card balance transfers allowed
- Existing personal loan BT considered on case-by-case basis

================================================================================
12. CONFLICTS/REVIEW ITEMS
================================================================================

None. Policy is deterministic with consistent values across categories.
