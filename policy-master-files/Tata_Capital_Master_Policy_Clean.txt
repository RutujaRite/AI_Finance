================================================================================
TATA CAPITAL PERSONAL LOAN - MASTER POLICY
================================================================================

BANK: Tata Capital Limited
PRODUCT: Personal Loan
PRIMARY PROFILE: Salaried

================================================================================
1. NORMAL SALARIED PERSONAL LOAN
================================================================================

Target Customer:
- Salaried profiles

Age:
- Minimum: 21 years
- Maximum: 58 years
- At last EMI, retirement age may be considered where applicable, subject to
  maximum age 65 years under the normal salaried policy

Residence Stability:
- Minimum 6 months in current residence

Employment Stability:
- Current employment: Minimum 12 months
- Total employment stability: 24 months
- If applicant age is >=26 years and current employment stability of 1 year is
  satisfied, 2-year stability proof may not be required
- Total job stability proof may be waived where customer has an eligible
  individual tradeline > Rs.1 Lakh opened more than 2 years ago, subject to policy

Minimum Net Salary:
- Tata Group Employee (TGE): Rs.15,000
- Super CAT A / CAT A: Rs.20,000
- CAT B / Government: Rs.25,000
- Unlisted Company: Rs.27,000 under the later updated policy

Minimum Loan Amount:
- Rs.75,000 under Normal Salaried policy

Maximum Loan Amount:
- Up to Rs.35 Lakhs for applicable categories
- CAT C / lower applicable category cap may be Rs.25 Lakhs as per normal policy table
- For Loan Amount > Rs.25 Lakhs:
  - Credit Life Insurance mandatory
  - No deviation on FOIR / multiplier
  - Minimum monthly salary: Rs.1.50 Lakhs

CIBIL:
- Normal salaried base policy: 725+
- Updated policy separately allows CIBIL 0 / -1 under applicable programs
- Do not apply CIBIL 0 / -1 to programs that explicitly exclude it

Tenure:
- Up to 72 months for eligible salary/category profiles
- CAT C normal salaried: Maximum 60 months
- Salary > Rs.30,000 required where 72-month rule applies

================================================================================
2. FOIR
================================================================================

Salary <= Rs.25,000:
- Maximum FOIR: 50%
- Maximum Unsecured FOIR: 40%

Salary > Rs.25,000 to Rs.50,000:
- Maximum FOIR: 60%
- Maximum Unsecured FOIR: 50%

Salary > Rs.50,000 to Rs.75,000:
- Maximum FOIR: 65%
- Maximum Unsecured FOIR: 55%

Salary > Rs.75,000:
- Maximum FOIR: 75%
- Maximum Unsecured FOIR: 65%

================================================================================
3. ABB
================================================================================

ABB rule applies to:
- CAT C
- Unapproved company category

ABB Requirement:
- ABB = 1 time proposed EMI
- Alternate banking in individual capacity may be included in ABB calculation

================================================================================
4. BONUS / ADDITIONAL INCOME
================================================================================

Salary Bonus:
- 70% of average of last 2 gross bonuses may be considered
- Bonus must be regular
- Annual or half-yearly bonus allowed
- Salary slips for bonus months required
- Bonus considered should not exceed 15% of NMI

Rental Income:
- Rental credit must reflect continuously for at least 6 months
- 60% of 6-month average monthly rental receipt may be considered
- Rental income should not exceed 20% of average net salary
- Rental Agreement required

================================================================================
5. CREDIT CARD / EXISTING LOAN RULES
================================================================================

Credit Card Outstanding:
- Cumulative CC outstanding should not exceed 6 times monthly salary at login
- CIBIL CC outstanding may be mitigated where valid proof establishes that
  actual outstanding is below 6 times monthly NTH

Active Tata Capital Personal Loans:
- Not more than 2 active PLs at underwriting
- 3rd loan to be treated as Top-Up where applicable

Recent Funding:
- No Tata Capital loan in last 3 months, excluding Credit Card
- For applicable internal pre-approved cases, recent-loan checks apply as per policy

CIBIL Velocity:
- More than 6 PL enquiries in last 30 days not accepted under later update
- Earlier one-pager states not more than 7 PL enquiries in last 30 days
- Use the latest active rule
- If more than 3 PL enquiries are present, recent bank statement may be required
  before disbursement as per policy

================================================================================
6. BALANCE TRANSFER
================================================================================

Maximum BT:
- Up to 6 BTs including Credit Card BT under normal salaried policy

Credit Card BT:
- Maximum 5 Credit Card BTs under detailed BT policy
- No late fee should be reported in Credit Card statement

Internal BT:
- Not considered under the detailed normal BT rule

BT Documents:
- Loan Account Statement
OR
- Loan Repayment Schedule + latest 3-month bank statement from which the loan
  has been repaid

OD / PL BT:
- OD to PL BT is allowed under the updated policy where applicable

================================================================================
7. LOW INCOME PROGRAM
================================================================================

Super CAT A / CAT A:
- Minimum NMI: Rs.15,000

CAT B / CAT C:
- Minimum NMI: Rs.20,000

Low-Income Band:
- Super CAT A / CAT A: Rs.15,000 to Rs.20,000
- CAT B / CAT C: Rs.20,000 to Rs.25,000

Loan Amount:
- Minimum: Rs.75,000
- Maximum: Rs.3 Lakhs
- For specified lower-income sub-band, maximum loan cap restricted to Rs.2 Lakhs

FOIR:
- Maximum FOIR: 50%
- Maximum Unsecured FOIR: 40%

Stability:
- Current Residence: 12 months
- Current Employment: 12 months
- Total Employment: 24 months

Not Allowed:

- Rented bachelor accommodation with permanent-address restriction as per policy

9. UNAPPROVED COMPANY EMPLOYEE PROGRAM
================================================================================

Minimum Loan Amount:
- Rs.1 Lakh

Maximum Loan Amount:
- Rs.10 Lakhs

Age:
- Minimum: 25 years
- Maximum: 58 years at loan maturity

Maximum Tenure:
- 60 months

Minimum Income:
- Loan < Rs.2 Lakhs: Rs.25,000

10. PROPRIETORSHIP / PARTNERSHIP EMPLOYER PROGRAM
================================================================================

This program is for salaried employees working with Proprietorship /
Partnership employers.

Loan Amount:
- Minimum: Rs.1 Lakh
- Maximum: Rs.7.5 Lakhs under the later Dec-2024 update

Income:
- Net Salary Rs.25,000: Maximum funding Rs.2 Lakhs
- Net Salary Rs.30,000+: Funding up to Rs.7.5 Lakhs

Age:
- Minimum: 25 years
- Maximum: 58 years

Tenure:
- 12 to 48 months

CIBIL:
- Minimum: 700+
- CIBIL -1: Not allowed

FOIR:
- Maximum: 50%

Employment:
- Minimum 1 year stability in same company
- Total employment / city stability requirements apply as per detailed policy

Banking / Income Verification:
- Salary credit through RTGS / NEFT
- Banking / Perfios required
- Form 16 required where applicable
- If Form 16 unavailable, Profile RCU may be required as stated in later update

Residence:
- For rented residence, Permanent FI mandatory
- Rented Bachelor Accommodation: Not allowed

ABB:
- Not applicable under the later Dec-2024 update

BT:
- Maximum 3 BTs
- Includes maximum 1 Credit Card BT

Not Allowed:
- CIBIL -1
- CCOD
- Rented Bachelor
- Negative Profiles

Pricing:
- ROI: 21% to 25%
- PF: 2%
- Insurance: Mandatory

12. CIBIL ADJUSTMENT OVER TERM-LOAN RAAC
================================================================================

For NTH bands below Rs.75K:
- CIBIL >=775: -0.25%
- CIBIL >=725 to <775: +0.00%
- CIBIL >=700 to <725: +0.25%
- CIBIL <700 including 0 / -1: +1.00%

For higher NTH bands shown in the rate matrix:
- CIBIL >=775: 0.00% where specified
- CIBIL >=725 to <775: 0.00%
- CIBIL >=700 to <725: +0.25%
- CIBIL <700 including 0 / -1: +1.00%

Always use the exact adjustment row corresponding to the selected NTH row.
