Golden Edge Program:

6. FOIR & MULTIPLIER RULES
===============================================================================

FOIR Limits:
- Standard FOIR: 75%
- Eligibility: 75% FOIR OR multiplier-based eligibility OR product cap whichever is lower
- Additional 3% FOIR for Government A-B & DA categories

Multiplier Grid:
- Multipliers as per company category
- Maximum loan amount as per company category: ₹5,00,000
- Additional income can be considered for increasing eligibility but not to extend FOIR benefit
- No OOP required for bonus calculation / addition
