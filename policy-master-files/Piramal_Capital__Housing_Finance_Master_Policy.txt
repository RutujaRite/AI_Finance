================================================================================
PIRAMAL FINANCE PERSONAL LOAN - MASTER POLICY

BANK: Piramal Finance
PRODUCT: Personal Loan

================================================================================
1. CORE PRODUCT RULES
================================================================================

Loan Amount:
- Standard Personal Loan: Rs. 1 Lakh to Rs. 25 Lakhs in one supplied policy summary.
- Revised/JFM material shows selected programs up to Rs. 30 Lakhs.
- Government Employees:
  - Fresh: Up to Rs. 20 Lakhs
  - Balance Transfer: Up to Rs. 24 Lakhs
- NTC / CIBIL -1:
  - Up to Rs. 7.5 Lakhs in revised JFM policy
  - Ventile-based cap may be lower; apply exact applicable policy section.

Tenure:
- Standard: 12 to 72 months
- Revised JFM program: up to 84 months for applicable profiles

Age:
- Minimum Age: 21 years
- Maximum Age: 61 years
- Government Employees: Maximum Age 63 years

Interest Rate:
- General policy range in supplied text: approximately 12.99% to 24.49%
- Exact ROI must be selected from the applicable NTC / Ventile / Employer Category pricing grid below.

Processing Fee:
- General policy: 1.50% to 2.50%
- Exact PL Prime flat PF rules are listed separately below.

Foreclosure / Part Payment:
- Nil / zero foreclosure charges in revised policy material
- Nil / zero part-payment charges in revised policy material
- No locking period stated in supplied policy text

================================================================================
2. ELIGIBLE EMPLOYER / PROFILE TYPES
================================================================================

Allowed:
- Private Limited company employees
- Government employees
- Partnership-firm employees
- Proprietorship employees
- NGO employees
- Society employees
- Trust employees
- LIC Agents
- Grade 4 profiles subject to applicable conditions

Partnership / Proprietorship:
- Own house required in supplied policy text
- Salary requirement appears as Rs. 30,000 in the later policy version

Government:
- Special loan caps apply
- Maximum age up to 63 years

================================================================================
3. MINIMUM INCOME / SALARY
================================================================================

Later supplied policy version:
- Minimum NTH: Rs. 27,500 per month
- PF deduction mandatory
- If PF is not deducted, Form 26AS mandatory

Partnership / Proprietorship:
- Minimum salary: Rs. 30,000
- Own house required

Older supplied policy variants also show:
- Rs. 20,000 general salary
- Rs. 22,000 for Partnership / Proprietorship

[REVIEW]
Use the currently active policy version. Do not mix older salary thresholds with newer policy rules.
