--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Policy 16-07-22:

  Credit-card outstanding up to an amount equivalent to NMI will not be obligated.

Any outstanding above that policy condition:
- Handle according to applicable obligation rules; do not infer a waiver beyond
  the stated amount.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.2 JOINT HOUSING LOAN OBLIGATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Policy 16-07-22:
- Joint Housing Loan obligation can be ignored if spouse is servicing the EMI
  from spouse's own account and has sufficient income to cover all EMIs serviced
  from that account.

Updated policy 07-11-24 similarly states:
- Joint HL only with spouse: HL obligation can be ignored when spouse pays the
  full HL and has sufficient income.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
10.3 VARIABLE ALLOWANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Updated policy 07-11-24:
- Consider the lowest of the last 3 months' variable allowance.
- Variable allowance must be part of all 3 months' payslips.

Do not use the highest or average variable allowance under this rule.
