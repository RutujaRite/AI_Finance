2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
================================================================================

CIBIL Score Requirements:
- Minimum CIBIL: 731
- PL CIBIL inquiry not more than 5 in last 30 days
- CAT D with CIBIL 750 and salary 75k: eligible for higher loan amount
- CIBIL <730 (Banking Surrogate Programme): Either office or residence must be owned
- NTC (New to Credit): Max loan amount ₹3,00,000

Age Requirements:
- Salaried: Minimum 21 years, Maximum 60 years
- SEP: Minimum 23 years, Maximum 65 years
- Non-Individual: No age limit
- Defense/Para-Military: Minimum 23 years, Maximum 56 years

Employment Types:
- Salaried
- Self Employed Professionals (SEP)
- Self Employed Non-Professionals (SENP)

Work Experience / Business Stability:
- Salaried: Minimum 1 year with at least 1 salary credit in new company
- Govt employee: 3 months
- Bandhan Bank Staff: Confirmed employees only
- SEP: Business vintage 1 year in same line of business
- SEP: Minimum 24 months at current address

Residence Stability:
- Minimum 6 months at current address (Salaried)
- Minimum 24 months at current address (SEP)
- Not required for self-owned residence

Customer Profile:
- Resident Indian
- Negative profile and Industry as per Internal List

3. COMPANY/EMPLOYER CATEGORIES
================================================================================

Company Categories:
- CAT A: Government employees, listed companies, A to AAA rated
- CAT B: Listed Pvt Ltd companies
- CAT C: Unlisted companies with 5 years MCA vintage, salary 40k+
- CAT D: Other salaried profiles
- SEP: Doctor, CA, CS, CWA, BDS, BHMS, BAMS, MBBS, Architect

Company Vintage Requirements:
- Unlisted company: 5 years in MCA
- Salary 40k can be sent for listing

Co-Applicant Rules:
- Proprietorship: Spouse/family member/blood relative
- Partnership: Partners with at least 51% stake mandatory
- Companies: Directors with at least 51% stake mandatory
- Class 4 Govt employees: Co-applicant mandatory
