8. BT / CCBT / APP LOAN RULES
================================================================================

Balance Transfer (BT) Rules:
- Pure BT and top-up allowed for Super CAT A
- PL Shift Plus: Takeover of Personal Loans with seasoning >= 1 month
- Existing BFL SALPL loan: Process in BTTP only, parallel funding not allowed
- CCBT: Max 4 cards can be clubbed
- CC outstanding >₹10L: Not allowed
- Live PL CS loan: Not allowed

Credit Card BT (CCBT):
- Program cap: ₹25 Cr (Prime), ₹10 Cr (Growth), ₹5 Cr (Prof SAL)
- CIBIL: >=720
- Allowed only in Prime market & G1 locations
- Late fee/over limit fee in last 3 months: Not allowed
- CC limit <₹1L: Not allowed
- CC outstanding <₹50k: Not allowed

App Loan Rules:
- For loan amount >= ₹5L: Income proof required
- For loan amount ₹3L-5L: No income proof required
- MTS 100% BT norms to be met
- No additional unsecured exposure in last 6 months (up to ₹50K loans not counted)
